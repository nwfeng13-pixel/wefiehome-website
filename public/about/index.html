About WefieHome关于 WefieHome

Professional short-term rental management with long-term property care, in Kuala Lumpur and Klang Valley.在吉隆坡及巴生谷，提供专业短租管理与长期物业照护。

Who we are我们是谁

WefieHome is a Kuala Lumpur-based short-term rental property management company operating across Kuala Lumpur and Klang Valley. We started our short-term rental business in March 2018 and have managed more than 20 properties since then.WefieHome 是一家总部位于吉隆坡的短租物业管理公司，业务覆盖吉隆坡及巴生谷。我们于 2018 年 3 月开始短租业务，至今已管理超过 20 个物业。

Today we manage 18 active units across six properties — condominiums in Kuala Lumpur and a landed house in Shah Alam. We handle guest communication, booking management, pricing, cleaning coordination, maintenance, property inspections and owner reporting.目前我们管理六个物业中的 18 个活跃单位 — 包括吉隆坡的公寓和莎阿南的一间有地住宅。我们负责住客沟通、预订管理、定价、清洁协调、维修、物业检查及业主报告。

WefieHome was founded by Ivan Ng, Founder & Managing Director. WefieHome is a brand operated by Think Bright PLT.WefieHome 由创始人兼董事经理 Ivan Ng 创立，是 Think Bright PLT 旗下品牌。

Questions about WefieHome关于 WefieHome 的常见问题

What is WefieHome?WefieHome 是什么公司？

WefieHome is a Kuala Lumpur-based short-term rental property management company operating across Kuala Lumpur and Klang Valley. It manages short-stay properties for owners and handles guest communication, booking management, pricing, cleaning coordination, maintenance, property inspections and owner reporting.WefieHome 是一家总部位于吉隆坡的短租物业管理公司，业务覆盖吉隆坡及巴生谷。我们为业主管理短租物业，负责住客沟通、预订管理、定价、清洁协调、维修、物业检查及业主报告。

How long has WefieHome been in short-term rental management?WefieHome 从事短租管理多久了？

WefieHome started its short-term rental business in March 2018 and has managed more than 20 properties since then.WefieHome 于 2018 年 3 月开始短租业务，至今已管理超过 20 个物业。

How many properties does WefieHome currently manage?WefieHome 目前管理多少物业？

WefieHome currently manages 18 active short-term rental units across six properties.WefieHome 目前管理六个物业中的 18 个活跃短租单位。

What is WefieHome's management fee?WefieHome 的管理费是多少？

WefieHome's current management model is a 20% management fee based on its defined gross-revenue calculation, with no upfront management fee. Exact contractual wording is set out in the management agreement.WefieHome 目前的管理模式为按定义总收入收取 20% 管理费，无前期管理费。具体条款以管理协议为准。

What is WefieHome's property-care philosophy?WefieHome 的物业照护理念是什么？

WefieHome aims to keep managed properties active, maintained and ready for use, rental or sale.WefieHome 致力让所管理的物业保持活跃、维护良好，随时可住、可租、可售。
