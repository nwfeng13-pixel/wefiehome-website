About WefieHome关于 WefieHome

Professional short-term rental management with long-term property care, in Kuala Lumpur and Klang Valley.在吉隆坡及巴生谷，提供专业短租管理与长期物业照护。

Who we are我们是谁

WefieHome is a Kuala Lumpur-based short-term rental property management company operating across Kuala Lumpur and Klang Valley. We started our short-term rental business in March 2018 and have managed more than 20 properties since then.WefieHome 是一家总部位于吉隆坡的短租物业管理公司，业务覆盖吉隆坡及巴生谷。我们于 2018 年 3 月开始短租业务，至今已管理超过 20 个物业。

Today we manage 18 active units across six properties — condominiums in Kuala Lumpur and a landed house in Shah Alam. We handle guest communication, booking management, pricing, cleaning coordination, maintenance, property inspections and owner reporting.目前我们管理六个物业中的 18 个活跃单位 — 包括吉隆坡的公寓和莎阿南的一间有地住宅。我们负责住客沟通、预订管理、定价、清洁协调、维修、物业检查及业主报告。

WefieHome was founded by Ivan Ng, Founder & Managing Director. WefieHome is a brand operated by Think Bright PLT.WefieHome 由创始人兼董事经理 Ivan Ng 创立，是 Think Bright PLT 旗下品牌。
