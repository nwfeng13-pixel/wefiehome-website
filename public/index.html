About WefieHome关于 WefieHome

WefieHome is a short-term rental property management company operating in Kuala Lumpur and Klang Valley since 2018. FAQ常见问题

Frequently asked questions常见问题

For owners业主

How much does WefieHome charge to manage my unit?WefieHome 的托管怎么收费？

WefieHome charges a management fee of 20% of defined gross revenue. Exact terms follow the signed management agreement.WefieHome 收取定义总收入的 20% 作为管理费，具体以签署的管理协议为准。

Is there an upfront fee?需要支付前期费用吗？

No. There is no upfront management fee — we earn when your unit earns.不需要。没有前期管理费——你的单位有收入，我们才有收入。

How long is the management agreement?管理协议期限多长？

The management agreement runs for 12 months and renews automatically. Exact terms follow the signed agreement.管理协议为期 12 个月，到期自动续约。具体以签署的协议为准。

How and when do owners get paid?业主如何、何时收到款项？

Owners receive a monthly payout together with a monthly report showing revenue and costs.业主每月收到分成款项，并附上列明收入与开支的月度报告。

What does WefieHome handle?WefieHome 负责哪些工作？

Assessment and setup advice, photography, listing creation and optimisation, pricing, guest communication and screening, check-in and check-out, cleaning and linen coordination, maintenance coordination, inspections, damage reporting and claims, and monthly owner reporting.评估与布置建议、拍照、房源建立与优化、定价、房客沟通与筛选、入住与退房、清洁与床品协调、维修协调、检查、损坏报告与索赔，以及每月业主报告。

Which areas do you cover?你们服务哪些地区？

Kuala Lumpur and the Klang Valley, including Cheras and Shah Alam.吉隆坡及巴生谷，包括蕉赖（Cheras）和莎阿南（Shah Alam）。

Who pays for cleaning, utilities, internet and repairs?清洁、水电、网络和维修费用由谁承担？

Operating expenses such as cleaning, utilities, internet and maintenance are discussed and agreed with the owner before the unit goes live. The arrangement depends on the unit and its operating needs, with all costs clearly outlined so you understand your responsibilities and expected expenses upfront.清洁、水电、网络和维修等运营开支，会在单位上线前与业主讨论并达成共识。具体安排视单位情况和运营需要而定，所有费用都会清楚列明，让你事先了解自己的责任和预期开支。

How long from signing until my unit is live for bookings?从签约到单位上线接受预订需要多久？

It depends on your unit's readiness, furnishing needs, photography and listing preparation. If your unit is already furnished and ready, it can be relatively quick; units that need more setup or improvements take longer. We coordinate the preparation with you and keep you informed before the listing goes live.这取决于单位的准备情况、家具需求、拍照和房源准备。如果单位已布置好、可以入住，过程会比较快；需要更多布置或改善的单位则需要更长时间。我们会与你协调准备工作，并在房源上线前随时告知进度。

Does my unit need furnishing or renovation, and who pays?单位需要添置家具或装修吗？费用由谁承担？

Not necessarily. We assess your unit's existing condition, furnishing and amenities before recommending any improvements. If more furnishing, renovation or upgrades are needed, we discuss the recommendations and estimated costs with you for approval before proceeding. Any investment and payment arrangements are agreed in advance.不一定。我们会先评估单位现有的状况、家具和设施，再提出改善建议。如需添置家具、装修或升级，我们会先与你讨论建议和预估费用，经你同意后才进行。所有投入和付款安排都会事先商定。

Can I block dates or stay in my own unit?业主可以保留日期或自己入住吗？

Yes, subject to availability and prior coordination. Let us know in advance so we can check existing bookings and arrange the dates. Owner stays are subject to confirmed guest reservations and the agreed management terms.可以，视空档情况并需提前协调。请提前通知我们，以便核对现有预订并安排日期。业主入住须以已确认的房客预订和双方约定的管理条款为准。

For guests住客

What are the check-in and check-out times?入住和退房时间是几点？

Check-in is usually from 3pm and check-out is by 12pm. The exact times are shown on your booking confirmation.入住时间一般为下午 3 点起，退房时间为中午 12 点前。确切时间以你的预订确认为准。

How do I book a WefieHome stay?如何预订 WefieHome 的住宿？

All WefieHome stays are booked on Airbnb. We have units at J Dupion Residence, M Vertica, Trion KL, Arte Cheras and Nexus Taman Pertama in Kuala Lumpur, and a landed house in Shah Alam Section 8.所有 WefieHome 住宿都通过 Airbnb 预订。我们在吉隆坡的 J Dupion Residence、M Vertica、Trion KL、Arte Cheras 和 Nexus Taman Pertama 设有单位，并在莎阿南第 8 区有一间有地住宅。

What are the house rules?有哪些入住规则？

Smoking is strictly not allowed in our units. Please follow the house rules shown on each listing.所有单位严禁吸烟。请遵守各房源列明的入住规则。
