How much does WefieHome charge for Airbnb management?WefieHome 的 Airbnb 托管如何收费？

Our management fee is 20% of defined gross revenue — the platform payout after applicable platform fees and cleaning fees, before our fee. There is no upfront management commission. Exact terms are set out in the management agreement.管理费为定义总收入的 20% — 即扣除平台费用及清洁费后、扣除我们管理费前的平台款项。无前期管理佣金。具体以管理协议为准。

What services are included in Airbnb management?Airbnb 托管包括哪些服务？

Listing setup, pricing strategy, guest communication, check-in and check-out, cleaning coordination, maintenance coordination, property inspections, monthly owner reporting and day-to-day operations.房源建立、定价策略、住客沟通、入住与退房、清洁协调、维修协调、物业检查、每月业主报告及日常运营。
