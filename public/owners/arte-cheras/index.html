Making a loft work for guests让阁楼适合房客

A loft is only an edge if it's set up for it.阁楼要布置对，才是优势。

Both Arte units we run today were furnished by their owners. Our job is to make them work for guests — here's what we look at in every loft.我们现在经营的两个 Arte 单位都是业主自己布置的。我们的工作是让它们适合房客——以下是我们在每个阁楼单位都会注意的地方。

01

Bed layout for 6为 6 人规划床位

We plan the beds across the lower bedrooms and the mezzanine so six guests sleep comfortably — not just a sofa bed in the corner.我们把床位分配在下层卧室和阁楼，让六位房客睡得舒服——而不是角落里一张沙发床。

02

Stairs & loft safety楼梯与阁楼安全

Open stairs and a mezzanine need extra attention. Railings, steps and lighting are part of our checks, and the house rules are clear for families with children.开放式楼梯和阁楼需要多留意。栏杆、台阶和照明都在我们的检查范围内，入住规则也会对带小孩的家庭说清楚。

03

Cool and bright upstairs楼上要凉、要亮

Heat rises in a double-height space. We look at how the aircon reaches the loft and whether extra fans or lighting are needed.挑高空间热气会往上走。我们会检查冷气能否照顾到阁楼，以及是否需要加风扇或灯光。

04

Photos that show the height拍出挑高感

A loft looks ordinary in a badly taken photo. Listing photos have to show both levels — that's what makes a guest choose your unit over the one next door.拍不好的阁楼看起来很普通。房源照片要同时拍出上下两层——这才是房客选你而不是隔壁那间的原因。

Are Arte Cheras loft units good for Airbnb?Arte Cheras 的阁楼单位适合做 Airbnb 吗？

They can be. The mezzanine lets a unit sleep a family or a group of friends, and the double height photographs well. Results depend on the set-up, the furnishing and the condition of the unit, so we assess each one first.可以。阁楼让单位能容纳一家人或一群朋友，挑高也很上镜。结果视布置、家具及单位状况而定，所以我们会先逐个评估。

My Arte unit is already furnished. Can WefieHome manage it?我的 Arte 单位已经布置好了，WefieHome 可以接手吗？
