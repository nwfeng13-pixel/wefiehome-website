Why a house is different洋房有什么不同

In a condo, the building looks after this. In a house, someone has to.在公寓，这些由管理处负责。在洋房，总得有人负责。

Most Airbnb operators only think about the inside. A landed home needs someone watching the whole property — especially when nobody lives there.大多数 Airbnb 业者只看室内。有地住宅需要有人照看整个物业——尤其是没有人住的时候。

No management office will do this for you:没有管理处会替你做：

  • Roof, gutters & walls屋顶、雨槽与外墙
  • Garden, drains & grass花园、排水沟与草地
  • Gate, locks & security大门、门锁与保安
  • Termites & pest control白蚁与虫害防治
  • Water tank, pump & wiring水箱、水泵与电线

Vacant doesn't mean risk-free.空置，不代表没有风险。

What kinds of landed homes do you take on?你们接哪些类型的有地住宅？

We focus on semi-detached houses and bungalows in Kuala Lumpur and the Klang Valley, and we also accept terrace houses. Every house starts with a site visit, because condition and location matter more than the type.我们主要接吉隆坡及巴生谷的半独立式和独立式洋房，也接排屋。每间房子都从实地考察开始，因为屋况和地点比类型更重要。

Can I still use my house?我还可以自己住吗？

Yes. Your house stays available for your own use when you need it. Just plan the dates with us and we keep those dates free.可以。需要时你随时可以使用房子，只需提前与我们协调日期，我们会把那些日期留给你。

Is the rental income guaranteed?租金收入有保证吗？

No. Income depends on actual bookings and costs. The aim is a house that is cared for, active and ready — with short-stay income helping to offset its running and upkeep costs.没有。收入取决于实际订单和开支。目标是让房子有人照顾、保持活跃、随时就绪——短租收入可帮助抵消日常开支和保养费用。

How much does WefieHome charge?WefieHome 怎么收费？
