Owner checklist业主清单

The Maxim Risen owner checklistMaxim Risen 业主清单

The essentials, from key collection to your first guest.从拿钥匙到迎接第一位房客的重点。

  1. 1

    Before key collection拿钥匙前

    Read your VP letter and payment notices; prepare ID, SPA copy and payment proof; plan who will inspect the unit with you.细读 VP 信和付款通知；准备身份证、SPA 副本和付款证明；安排好陪同验屋的人。

  2. 2

    At handover交屋当天

    Record meter readings; collect all keys and access cards; photograph every room before anything changes.记录水电表读数；领齐所有钥匙和门卡；改动前先把每个房间拍照存档。

  3. 3

    Defect inspection瑕疵验收

    Check walls, tiles, doors, windows, plumbing, sockets and water pressure; submit defects in writing within the defect liability period and keep copies.检查墙面、瓷砖、门窗、水管、插座和水压；在保修期内以书面提交瑕疵并保留副本。

  4. 4

    Before renovating装修前

    Get management approval and know the renovation rules; decide your rental route first so you only build what earns.取得管理处批准并了解装修规定；先定出租方式，只做能带来回报的装修。

  5. 5

    Furnishing添置家具

    Furnish for your target guest; durable, easy-to-clean pieces; beds and bathrooms first, décor last.按目标客群布置；选耐用、易清洁的家具；床和浴室优先，装饰最后。

  6. 6

    Before your first guest第一位房客入住前

    Utilities and Wi-Fi active; safety items in place; professional photos; listing, pricing and house rules ready.水电和 Wi-Fi 开通；安全用品到位；专业拍照；房源、定价和入住规则准备好。
