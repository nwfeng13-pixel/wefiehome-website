J Dupion owner FAQsJ Dupion 业主常见问题

Who manages Airbnb units at J Dupion Residence?谁在 J Dupion Residence 托管 Airbnb 单位？

WefieHome is an Airbnb management company that has operated at J Dupion Residence (JD Residence), Cheras, since 2018. We currently manage 7 units in the building: six 2-bedroom units and one 3-bedroom unit.WefieHome 是一家 Airbnb 托管公司，自 2018 年起在 Cheras 的 J Dupion Residence（JD Residence）运营，目前在这栋楼管理 7 个单位：六间两房和一间三房。

How long has WefieHome been operating at J Dupion?WefieHome 在 J Dupion 运营多久了？

Since 2018, the year the building was completed. We know the building, its facilities and the guests it attracts.从 2018 年大楼竣工起至今。我们熟悉这栋楼、它的设施，以及它吸引的房客。

How much does WefieHome charge?WefieHome 怎么收费？

A 20% management fee with no upfront management fee — we earn when your unit earns. WhatsApp us for the full terms and what's included.20% 管理费，没有前期管理费——你的单位有收入，我们才有收入。完整条款及服务内容，请 WhatsApp 咨询。

Which J Dupion layout works best for Airbnb?J Dupion 哪种户型最适合做 Airbnb？

The 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom units (about 800 sq ft) are the most flexible and are what we run most. The 3-bedroom units suit larger families and groups. Larger duplex, triplex and penthouse units are assessed case by case.两房两浴单位（约 800 平方尺）最灵活，也是我们经营最多的户型。三房单位适合大家庭和团体。较大的复式、三层及顶楼单位需逐个评估。

My unit is already furnished. Can WefieHome manage it?我的单位已经布置好了，WefieHome 可以接手吗？

Yes. We review the unit first and tell you honestly if anything should be added or changed for guests. Many units only need small adjustments and good photos.可以。我们会先看单位，坦白告诉你是否需要为房客添置或调整什么。很多单位只需小调整和好照片。

Do I need to live in Kuala Lumpur?我需要住在吉隆坡吗？

No. Many of our owners are busy with work, family or business, or live outside KL. You can reach us on WhatsApp, and you receive a monthly report.不需要。我们很多业主工作、家庭或生意忙碌，或住在吉隆坡以外。有事 WhatsApp 我们，每月也会收到报告。

How will I know how my unit is performing?我怎么知道单位的经营状况？

You receive a monthly owner statement showing bookings, gross revenue, cleaning fees, management fee, repairs, purchases, utilities and your payout.你每月会收到业主报表，列明订单、总收入、清洁费、管理费、维修、采购、水电费以及你的分成。

Is WefieHome connected to the J Dupion developer or building management?WefieHome 和 J Dupion 的发展商或管理处有关系吗？

No. WefieHome is an independent short-term rental management company operating in Kuala Lumpur and Klang Valley since 2018.没有。WefieHome 是一家独立的短租管理公司，自 2018 年起在吉隆坡及巴生谷运营。
