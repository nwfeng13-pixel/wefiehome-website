M Vertica owner FAQsM Vertica 业主常见问题

Who manages Airbnb units at M Vertica (MV)?谁在 M Vertica（MV）托管 Airbnb 单位？

WefieHome is an Airbnb management company that has managed short-stay units at M Vertica, Cheras since 2022. We currently look after 5 three-bedroom units in the development.WefieHome 是一家 Airbnb 托管公司，自 2022 年起在 Cheras 的 M Vertica 托管短租单位，目前照顾 5 个三房单位。

Do I need to do anything once I hand my unit over?把单位交给你们之后，我还需要做什么吗？

Very little. We handle pricing, guest messages, check-ins, cleaning, inspections, repairs coordination and reviews. You receive a monthly statement on WhatsApp, and we contact you only for decisions that need you, such as larger repairs.很少。定价、房客沟通、入住、清洁、检查、维修协调和评价都由我们处理。你每月在 WhatsApp 收到报表；只有需要你决定的事（例如较大的维修）我们才会联系你。

How do you keep my unit in good condition?你们怎么保持我的单位状况良好？

The unit is cleaned and checked after every stay, with routine inspections of aircon, appliances and fixtures. Small issues are fixed before the next guest arrives. Our M Vertica units have been hosting since 2022 and are still kept in good condition.每次退房后都会清洁和检查，并定期检查冷气、电器和各项设施；小问题会在下一位房客入住前处理好。我们在 M Vertica 的单位自 2022 年起接待房客，至今仍保持良好状况。

How much does WefieHome charge?WefieHome 怎么收费？

A 20% management fee with no upfront management fee — we earn when your unit earns. WhatsApp us for full terms and what's included.20% 管理费，没有前期管理费——你的单位有收入，我们才有收入。完整条款及服务内容，请 WhatsApp 咨询。

How will I know how my unit is performing?我怎么知道单位的经营状况？

Every month you receive an owner statement showing bookings, gross revenue, cleaning fees, management fee, repairs, purchases, utilities and your payout.你每月会收到业主报表，列明订单、总收入、清洁费、管理费、维修、采购、水电费以及你的分成。

Who stays in M Vertica short-term rentals?谁会入住 M Vertica 的短租单位？

Mostly overseas travellers, local family groups and business travellers. M Vertica is about a 7-minute walk from Maluri MRT and close to Sunway Velocity Mall, which suits all three.主要是海外旅客、本地家庭团体和商务客。M Vertica 步行约 7 分钟到 Maluri MRT，也靠近 Sunway Velocity 购物中心，三类客群都适合。

Does a 4-bedroom M Vertica unit work for Airbnb too?M Vertica 的四房单位也适合做 Airbnb 吗？

Yes. Our M Vertica units are 3-bedroom, but the 4-bedroom layout suits larger family groups. We assess every unit before making a proposal.适合。我们目前在 M Vertica 经营的是三房单位，四房户型适合更大的家庭团体。我们会先评估每个单位再给出方案。

Is WefieHome connected to Mah Sing or the M Vertica building management?WefieHome 和 Mah Sing 或 M Vertica 管理处有关系吗？

No. WefieHome is an independent short-term rental management company operating in Kuala Lumpur and Klang Valley since 2018.没有。WefieHome 是一家独立的短租管理公司，自 2018 年起在吉隆坡及巴生谷运营。
