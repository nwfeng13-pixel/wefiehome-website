Making a view work for guests让景色为房客所用

A view only sells if the room is set up for it.房间布置对了，景色才卖得出去。

We furnished and styled our 3-bedroom at Trion ourselves; the 2-bedroom was furnished by its owner. Either way, here's what we look at in every high-floor unit.我们在 Trion 的三房单位由我们自己布置和设计，两房单位则由业主布置。不管哪一种，以下是我们在每个高楼层单位都会注意的地方。

01

Face the room to the view让房间朝向景色

Sofa, dining table and beds are placed so guests see the skyline from where they actually sit and sleep — not with their backs to it.沙发、餐桌和床的位置都要让房客坐着、躺着就能看到天际线——而不是背对着它。

02

Curtains that work both ways窗帘要两头兼顾

Full-height glass is the selling point by day and a problem at 7am. Sheers for the view, blackout for sleep.落地玻璃白天是卖点，早上七点却会刺眼。纱帘留景，遮光帘保证睡眠。

03

Photos timed for the skyline照片要拍到天际线

A view photo taken in flat light looks like any other window. Listing photos are shot so the landmarks read clearly.光线不好时拍的景观照，看起来和普通窗户没两样。房源照片要拍得让地标一眼认得出。

04

Balcony rules, high up高楼阳台规则

A high-floor balcony needs clear house rules — especially for families with children — and we check it at every changeover.高楼阳台需要清楚的入住规则——尤其是带小孩的家庭——每次换房我们都会检查。

Who manages Airbnb units at Trion KL?谁在 Trion KL 托管 Airbnb 单位？

WefieHome, an independent Airbnb management company in Kuala Lumpur, has managed short-stay units at Trion KL since 2023. We currently run 2 units there: a 3-bedroom usually set up for 6 guests and a 2-bedroom usually set up for 4.WefieHome 是吉隆坡一家独立的 Airbnb 托管公司，自 2023 年起在 Trion KL 经营短租单位，目前管理 2 个单位：一间通常可住 6 人的三房，以及一间通常可住 4 人的两房。

Does a skyline view really matter on Airbnb?天际线景观在 Airbnb 上真的重要吗？

It helps a listing stand out, because guests can see it in the photos before they book. But a view only works if the room is set up and photographed for it — and views at Trion depend on the block, the side and the floor. We look at your unit before saying what it can do.景观能让房源更突出，因为房客订房前就能在照片里看到。但房间要为景观布置、照片也要拍出来，景才有用——而且 Trion 的景观视大楼、朝向和楼层而定。我们会先看你的单位，再说它能有什么表现。

Can WefieHome furnish my Trion unit?WefieHome 可以帮我布置 Trion 单位吗？

Yes. We furnished and styled the 3-bedroom we run at Trion. The 2-bedroom was furnished by its owner, and we manage that one as it is. Either way works.可以。我们在 Trion 经营的三房单位就是我们布置和设计的。两房单位由业主自己布置，我们按现状经营。两种方式都可以。

How do guests get to Trion KL?房客怎么去 Trion KL？

Chan Sow Lin LRT and MRT station is within walking distance, and a shuttle bus also runs to the station. There are shops, a café, restaurants and convenience stores downstairs.Chan Sow Lin LRT 及 MRT 站步行可达，另有接驳巴士前往车站。楼下有商店、咖啡馆、餐厅和便利店。
