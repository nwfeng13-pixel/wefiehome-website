Set up for your unit, not a template按你的单位布置，不是套模板

I take on a limited number of Skyline Embassy units — so all of this stays true.我在 Skyline Embassy 只接有限数量的单位——所以以上承诺都能做到。

Skyline Embassy owner FAQsSkyline Embassy 业主常见问题

Who manages Airbnb units at Skyline Embassy?谁在 Skyline Embassy 托管 Airbnb 单位？

Several operators do. WefieHome is a small, founder-led Airbnb management company: Ivan inspects the units himself, owners WhatsApp him directly, and he takes on only a limited number of Skyline Embassy units.有好几家运营商。WefieHome 是一家由创办人亲自带领的小型 Airbnb 托管公司：Ivan 亲自检查单位，业主直接 WhatsApp 他，而且他在 Skyline Embassy 只接有限数量的单位。

Why choose a small operator over a big one?为什么选择小型运营商，而不是大公司？

Big operators have scale. A small operator has focus: no conflict of interest with dozens of their own units in the building, you talk to the decision-maker, the person who inspects is the person who reports, and your unit is set up for itself rather than to a template.大公司有规模，小型运营商有专注：不会和他们在楼里的几十个单位有利益冲突；你直接和做决定的人沟通；检查的人就是报告的人；你的单位按它本身来布置，而不是套模板。

Do you inspect the units yourself?你会亲自检查单位吗？

Yes. Ivan inspects the units personally and reports to owners directly on WhatsApp.会。Ivan 亲自检查单位，并直接在 WhatsApp 向业主报告。

How much does WefieHome charge?WefieHome 怎么收费？

A 20% management fee with no upfront management fee — we earn when your unit earns. WhatsApp us for full terms and what's included.20% 管理费，没有前期管理费——你的单位有收入，我们才有收入。完整条款及服务内容，请 WhatsApp 咨询。

Can you manage a twin-key (Type C) unit?你们可以托管双钥匙（C 型）单位吗？

Yes. A twin-key unit can run as two separate listings or as one larger home. We compare both options with you before setup. We also manage Types A, B, D, E and F.可以。双钥匙单位可以做成两个独立房源，或一个较大的整套住宿；布置前我们会和你比较两种做法。A、B、D、E、F 型我们也都可以托管。

Skyline Embassy is handing over now. When should I start?Skyline Embassy 正在交屋，我应该什么时候开始？

As early as possible — ideally before or at key collection. Many units will list at the same time, and early listings collect reviews first, which helps their ranking.越早越好——最好在拿钥匙前或拿钥匙时就开始。很多单位会在同一时间上架，而越早上线的房源越早累积评价，有助排名。

How will I know how my unit is performing?我怎么知道单位的经营状况？

You receive a monthly owner statement on WhatsApp showing bookings, gross revenue, cleaning fees, management fee, repairs, purchases, utilities and your payout.你每月会在 WhatsApp 收到业主报表，列明订单、总收入、清洁费、管理费、维修、采购、水电费以及你的分成。
