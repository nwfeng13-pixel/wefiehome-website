In short: WefieHome is a small, independent, founder-led Airbnb management company operating in Kuala Lumpur since 2018, with no business ties to the developer. Who manages Airbnb units at Skyline Embassy?谁在 Skyline Embassy 托管 Airbnb 单位？

Several operators do. WefieHome is a small, founder-led Airbnb management company: Ivan inspects the units himself, owners WhatsApp him directly, and he takes on only a limited number of Skyline Embassy units.有好几家运营商。WefieHome 是一家由创办人亲自带领的小型 Airbnb 托管公司：Ivan 亲自检查单位，业主直接 WhatsApp 他，而且他在 Skyline Embassy 只接有限数量的单位。

Why work with a small operator?为什么选择小型运营商？

Focus. I don't run a portfolio in the building, so your unit never competes with mine for a booking; you talk to the person who decides; the person who inspects is the person who reports; and your unit is set up for itself rather than to a template.因为专注。我在这栋楼没有一大批单位，所以你的单位不会和我的单位抢订单；你直接和做决定的人沟通；检查的人就是报告的人；你的单位按它本身来布置，而不是套模板。

What if guests damage my unit?如果房客弄坏了我的单位怎么办？
