A plain balcony, turned into a garden corner普通阳台，变成一角小花园

The view was always there, but the balcony gave guests little reason to step outside. We turned it into a small garden corner for morning coffee and evening drinks.景色一直都在，但阳台本身没什么让房客想走出去的理由。我们把它改造成一个小花园角落，适合早上喝咖啡、晚上小酌。

Before之前 · 2024After之后 · 2026

What we did我们做了什么

  • Vertical plant wall along the side wall侧墙加装垂直绿植墙
  • Patterned floor tiles over the plain floor原本素面地板铺上花砖
  • Bar-height counter and chairs facing the view面向景色的吧台和高脚椅

Why it matters to the owner对业主的意义

Listing photos are the first thing guests compare. A balcony guests want to sit on makes the photos — and the stay — stand out from other units in the same building.房源照片是房客比较的第一步。一个让人想坐下来的阳台，能让照片和住宿体验都在同一栋楼的众多单位中脱颖而出。

A bare balcony, made for slow evenings空荡的阳台，变成慢享傍晚的地方

A large 3-bedroom unit, popular with families and groups. Its balcony faced the KL skyline but was completely empty. We made it a place to sit together.这是一间宽敞的三房单位，很受家庭和团体欢迎。阳台面向吉隆坡天际线，却完全空着。我们把它变成大家可以坐在一起的地方。

What we did我们做了什么

  • Timber decking over the bare floor原本素面地板铺上木地板
  • Rattan lounge chairs and a small table藤编休闲椅和小桌
  • Plants, shelves and warm string lights for the evening绿植、层架和傍晚用的暖色串灯

Why it matters to the owner对业主的意义

Groups book larger units for time together. An outdoor space that works day and night gives them one more reason to choose this unit — without changing the layout.团体选择大单位，是为了相聚。一个白天晚上都能用的户外空间，让他们多一个选择这间的理由——而且不用改动格局。

Looking after a landed family home照顾一间有庭院的家庭住宅

A landed house needs more care than a condo: garden, roof, gates, more rooms and more fittings. We run it as a short stay for families and keep the whole house in guest-ready condition.独立洋房需要比公寓更多的照顾：花园、屋顶、大门、更多房间和设备。我们把它作为家庭短租经营，同时让整间房子保持随时可接待房客的状态。

What we did我们做了什么

  • Run as a short stay for families and big groups作为家庭和团体短租经营
  • Cleaned, restocked and checked after every stay每次退房后清洁、补货和检查
  • Upkeep issues handled and reported to the owner维修保养问题及时处理并向业主汇报

Why it matters to the owner对业主的意义

The owner's house is lived in, looked after and earning — instead of sitting empty. Guests notice the care, and say so in their reviews.业主的房子有人住、有人照顾，也在产生收入——而不是空置着。房客感受得到这份用心，也会在评价里提到。
