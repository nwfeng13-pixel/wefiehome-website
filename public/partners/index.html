Partner FAQs合作常见问题

What does a partnership with WefieHome look like?和 WefieHome 合作是怎样的？

Every partnership is different, so we discuss it one-to-one with Ivan. The aim is simple: a long-term, win-win relationship where your clients are well looked after and both sides grow.每一种合作都不一样，所以我们会和 Ivan 一对一详谈。目标很简单：长期、双赢的关系——你的客户得到妥善照顾，双方一起成长。

Which areas do you cover?你们服务哪些地区？

Kuala Lumpur and the Klang Valley. Homes outside the areas we operate in today are welcome too — we'll discuss each one.吉隆坡及巴生谷。目前还没经营的地区也欢迎——我们会逐个讨论。

Do you take condos and landed homes?公寓和有地住宅都接吗？

Yes, both. We run condominium units across Kuala Lumpur and a landed house in Shah Alam, and every home starts with an assessment.都接。我们在吉隆坡经营多个公寓单位，在莎阿南经营一间有地住宅；每个物业都从评估开始。

Can I introduce a unit in a building you're not in yet?可以介绍你们还没进驻的楼盘单位吗？

Yes. Tell us about the unit and we'll look at it together.可以。告诉我们单位的情况，我们一起看看。

What if the owner decides not to go ahead?如果业主最后决定不做呢？

That's fine. We give an honest view and leave the decision to the owner. If a home doesn't suit short stays, we'll say so.没问题。我们会如实给出意见，决定权在业主。如果房子不适合做短租，我们会直说。
