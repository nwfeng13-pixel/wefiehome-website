Quick answer快速解答

Most Airbnb management companies in Kuala Lumpur charge a percentage of rental revenue, usually 15% to 25%. WefieHome charges 20% of defined gross revenue, with no upfront management fee. The percentage is only part of the cost, though. What matters is how "revenue" is defined and which costs stay with the owner.吉隆坡大部分 Airbnb 代管公司按租金收入收取百分比，一般在 15% 至 25%。WefieHome 收取定义总收入的 20%，不收任何预付代管费。不过，百分比只是其中一部分，更关键的是“收入”如何定义，以及哪些费用由业主承担。

What "20% of gross revenue" means at WefieHomeWefieHome 的“20% 总收入”是什么意思

Gross revenue at WefieHome is the Airbnb payout after Airbnb's fees and after cleaning fees, before WefieHome's management fee. The 20% is charged on that figure.WefieHome 的总收入是指：Airbnb 打款金额扣除 Airbnb 服务费及清洁费之后、扣除 WefieHome 代管费之前的金额，代管费按此收取 20%。

  • No upfront management fee. WefieHome earns when the property earns.无预付代管费。 物业有收入，WefieHome 才有收入。
  • 12-month agreement that renews automatically.12 个月合约，到期自动续约。
  • Monthly owner payout, usually by the 15th of the following month, with a revenue report.每月结算，一般在次月 15 日前付款，并附收入报告。

  • Maintenance coordination, property inspections, and damage reporting and claims维修协调、定期巡查、损坏报告及索赔
  • Review management and owner reporting评价管理及业主报告
  • Routine replenishment of linen, shampoo, cleaning agents and minor consumables日常补货：床品、洗发水、清洁剂及小型消耗品

What the owner still pays业主仍需承担的费用

Property-related expenses stay with the owner. These usually include:与物业相关的开支由业主承担，一般包括：

  • Condo maintenance fees and sinking fund, quit rent and assessment公寓管理费及偏债基金、地税及门牌税
  • Electricity, water and internet电费、水费及网络
  • Repairs and replacement of furniture, appliances and fittings家具、电器及装修的维修与更换
  • The initial furnishing or renovation, if the unit is not ready yet尚未布置的单位所需的家具或装修
  • The mortgage, if there is one房贷（如有）

How to compare managers如何比较代管公司

  1. Ask how "revenue" is defined, in writing.要求以书面说明“收入”的定义。
  2. List every cost the owner pays, including consumables and laundry.列出业主需承担的所有费用，包括消耗品和洗衣。
  3. Ask for a sample monthly owner report.索取一份每月业主报告样本。
  4. Check the agreement length, the notice period and what happens at the end.确认合约期限、通知期及合约结束后的安排。
  5. Ask how the manager protects the property: inspections, guest screening and damage claims.了解代管公司如何保护物业：巡查、房客筛选及损坏索赔。
