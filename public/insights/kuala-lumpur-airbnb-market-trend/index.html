Quick answer快速解答

Kuala Lumpur has roughly 18,500 to 20,000 active Airbnb listings, depending on the data provider. Supply has grown 27–38% in the past year and more than doubled in three years. Average revenue per listing has not kept pace, so competition is rising and well-run listings are pulling ahead of the rest.吉隆坡目前约有 18,500 至 20,000 个活跃 Airbnb 房源（不同数据平台统计略有差异）。房源数量在过去一年增长 27–38%，三年内翻倍有余。但每个房源的平均收入并未同步增长，竞争正在加剧，经营得好的房源与其他房源的差距也在拉大。

What the KL market looks like吉隆坡市场概况

These figures are from AirROI (Aug 2025 – Jul 2026):以下数据来自 AirROI（2025 年 8 月 – 2026 年 7 月）：

  • Mostly condos: about 96% of listings are apartments or condos (AirROI).以公寓为主： 约 96% 的房源是公寓（AirROI）。
  • Mostly small units: about 70% are 1- or 2-bedroom units, so larger homes for families and groups are a smaller share of supply (AirROI).以小单位为主： 约 70% 为一房或两房，适合家庭和团体的大单位较少（AirROI）。
  • Seasons: December is the strongest month and November the weakest (AirROI).季节性： 12 月最旺，11 月最淡（AirROI）。

With more listings competing for guests, an average listing is easy to overlook. Pricing, photos, reviews, cleanliness and fast responses are what move a unit towards the top group.房源越多，普通房源越容易被忽略。定价、照片、评价、清洁和回复速度，决定一个单位能否进入前列。

What the trend means for owners趋势对业主的意义

  1. Location and building matter more. Units near MRT/LRT, city attractions, hospitals and business districts face a wider guest pool.地点和公寓更关键。 靠近 MRT/LRT、市区景点、医院和商业区的单位，客源更广。
  2. Larger units face less competition. With about 70% of supply in 1–2 bedroom units, well-presented 3-bedroom units and landed homes serve families and groups that have fewer options.大单位竞争较少。 约 70% 的房源是一至两房，布置良好的三房单位和有地住宅可服务选择较少的家庭和团体。
  3. Management quality shows in the numbers. The occupancy gap between the top and bottom quarters is roughly 40 percentage points.管理水平反映在数字上。 前 25% 与后 25% 的入住率相差约 40 个百分点。
  4. Projections should be cautious. A fair estimate uses the realistic middle of the market for that building, not the top 10%.收入预估应保守。 合理的估算应参考该公寓市场的中间水平，而不是前 10%。

Frequently asked questions常见问题

How many Airbnb listings are there in Kuala Lumpur?吉隆坡有多少 Airbnb 房源？

About 18,500 to 20,000 active listings in 2026, according to AirROI and Airbtics.根据 AirROI 和 Airbtics，2026 年约有 18,500 至 20,000 个活跃房源。

Is the KL Airbnb market saturated?吉隆坡 Airbnb 市场是否已饱和？

Supply is growing faster than revenue per listing, so competition is rising. Well-run listings in good locations still reach high occupancy.房源增长快于每房源收入增长，竞争正在加剧；但地点好、经营好的房源仍能达到高入住率。

What type of property is most common?哪类物业最常见？

Condos and apartments make up about 96% of listings, mostly 1- and 2-bedroom units.公寓约占 96%，以一房和两房为主。

Is Airbnb legal in Kuala Lumpur?在吉隆坡做 Airbnb 合法吗？

Short-term rental is not banned in KL, but owners must follow DBKL registration and their building's strata by-laws, which can prohibit it. National guidelines are being finalised.吉隆坡并未禁止短租，但业主须遵守 DBKL 登记要求及公寓分层管理规则（规则可禁止短租）。全国指南正在定稿。
