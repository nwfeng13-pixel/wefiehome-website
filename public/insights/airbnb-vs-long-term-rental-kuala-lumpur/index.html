Quick answer快速解答

Airbnb can earn more than a long-term tenancy in Kuala Lumpur, but the income moves month to month and needs active management. A long-term tenancy gives steady, predictable rent with less work. Which suits you depends on your building's rules, the location, how ready the unit is, and how much income variation you can live with.在吉隆坡，Airbnb 短租的收入有可能高于长租，但每月收入会波动，也需要持续管理。长租则收入稳定、较省心。哪种更适合，取决于公寓规定、地点、单位的布置情况，以及您能接受多大的收入波动。

When Airbnb tends to make sense适合做 Airbnb 的情况

  • The building allows short-term rental, and the JMB/MC by-laws do not prohibit it.公寓允许短租，JMB/MC 管理规则没有禁止。
  • The location draws travellers: near MRT/LRT, city attractions, hospitals, universities or business districts.地点吸引旅客：靠近 MRT/LRT、市区景点、医院、大学或商业区。
  • The unit is, or can be, furnished to a standard guests will rate well.单位已经或可以布置到房客满意的水平。
  • You want the option to use the unit yourself or keep it ready for sale.您希望保留自用或随时出售的灵活性。
  • You can accept income that changes from month to month.您能接受每月收入有高有低。

When long-term rental tends to make sense适合长租的情况

  • The building's by-laws restrict or ban short-term stays.公寓管理规则限制或禁止短租。
  • You need a fixed amount every month, for example to match a loan repayment exactly.您需要每月固定收入，例如刚好对应房贷还款。
  • The location has strong tenant demand but few visitors.地点租客需求高但旅客少。
  • You do not want to invest in furnishing.您不想投入家具布置。

Rules to check first先了解相关规定

In Kuala Lumpur, a building's strata by-laws matter most. Malaysia's Federal Court has held that management corporations can validly prohibit short-term rentals through house rules made under the Strata Management Act 2013. DBKL also expects short-term rental operators to register. National short-term rental accommodation (STRA) guidelines are still at final review as of October 2026. For more on the market and rules, see How many Airbnbs are in Kuala Lumpur.在吉隆坡，公寓的分层管理规则最为关键。马来西亚联邦法院已裁定，管理机构依据《2013 年分层管理法令》制定的内规可以合法禁止短租。DBKL 也要求短租经营者登记。截至 2026 年 10 月，全国短租住宿（STRA）指南仍在最后审查阶段。更多市场及法规资讯，请参阅吉隆坡有多少 Airbnb。

Property care: the part owners often overlook物业保养：业主常忽略的一点

A long-term tenant lives in the unit for a year or more, and problems may only surface at move-out. A managed short-term rental is cleaned and checked after every stay, so small issues get found and fixed early. WefieHome's approach is to keep managed properties active, maintained and ready to use, rent or sell.长租租客一住就是一年或更久，问题往往到搬离时才发现。代管的短租单位每次退房后都会清洁和检查，小问题能及早发现和修复。WefieHome 的理念是让物业保持活跃、保养良好，随时可自用、出租或出售。

Frequently asked questions常见问题

Is Airbnb more profitable than long-term rental in KL?在吉隆坡做 Airbnb 比长租更赚钱吗？

It can be, in the right building and location, but the income varies. Compare a realistic average month, after management fees and utilities, against the long-term rent you could get.在合适的公寓和地点有可能，但收入会波动。应以扣除代管费和水电后的合理平均月收入，与可收取的长租租金比较。

Can I switch from long-term rental to Airbnb later?长租以后可以转做 Airbnb 吗？

Yes, once the tenancy ends, if the building allows short-term stays and the unit is furnished to guest standard.可以。租约结束后，只要公寓允许短租、单位布置达到房客标准即可。

Does Airbnb cause more wear and tear?Airbnb 会造成更多损耗吗？

Guest turnover adds use, but regular cleaning, inspections and quick repairs help keep the unit in good condition.房客轮替会增加使用量，但定期清洁、巡查和及时维修有助保持单位状况良好。

Yes. A property assessment looks at the building, location, unit condition and likely demand before any agreement.可以。物业评估会在签约前考察公寓、地点、单位状况及预期需求。
