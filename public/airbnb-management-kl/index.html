FAQ

Frequently asked questions常见问题

What does an Airbnb management company do?Airbnb 托管公司负责什么？

It operates a short-stay property on the owner's behalf. At WefieHome this covers property assessment and setup, listing and pricing, guest communication and booking management, check-in and check-out, cleaning and linen coordination, maintenance coordination, property inspections and monthly owner reporting.代业主运营短租物业。WefieHome 的服务包括物业评估与布置、房源与定价、住客沟通与预订管理、入住退房、清洁与床品协调、维修协调、物业检查及每月业主报告。

How much does WefieHome charge?WefieHome 如何收费？

A 20% management fee with revenue sharing, and no upfront management fee — we earn when your property earns. The full terms are explained during your property assessment and set out in the management agreement.20% 管理费，收益分成，无前期管理费——你的物业有收入，我们才有收入。完整条款会在物业评估时说明，并列明在管理协议中。

Is there an upfront management commission?有前期管理佣金吗？

No. There is no upfront management commission. Setup, furnishing or renovation costs, if needed, are assessed and discussed with you before the agreement.没有。如需布置、家具或装修，相关费用会在签约前评估并与您讨论。

Is my property suitable for Airbnb?我的房产适合做 Airbnb 吗？

It depends on the location, unit type, condition, furnishing and the building's requirements. Request a property assessment and we'll review your unit and discuss its suitability with you.这取决于地点、户型、状况、家具及大楼规定。申请物业评估，我们会查看您的单位并与您讨论是否适合。

What is the owner responsible for?业主需要负责什么？

Property-related expenses are borne by the owner — for example repairs, purchases, electricity and water. WefieHome handles routine replenishment such as linen, shampoo, cleaning agents and minor consumables.物业相关费用由业主承担 — 例如维修、采购、电费和水费。床品、洗发水、清洁剂及小型耗材等日常补给由 WefieHome 负责。

How does WefieHome handle maintenance?WefieHome 如何处理维修？

We assess the issue, inform the owner, coordinate the repair or replacement, and check that the work has been completed.我们评估问题、通知业主、协调维修或更换，并确认工作已完成。

How does WefieHome screen guests?WefieHome 如何审核住客？

As applicable, we check guest verification, profile, reviews and history, the number of guests, communication, and acknowledgement of house rules.视情况核查住客的验证状态、个人资料、评价与记录、入住人数、沟通情况，以及是否确认房规。

How often is the property inspected?物业多久检查一次？

The cleaner checks the property after every checkout, and supervisors carry out random checks.每次退房后由清洁人员检查，并由主管进行不定期抽查。

What reports does the owner receive?业主会收到什么报告？

A monthly report covering gross revenue, cleaning fees, WefieHome management fees, repairs, purchases, electricity, water and the owner payout.每月报告，包括总收入、清洁费、WefieHome 管理费、维修、采购、电费、水费及业主分账。

How long is the management agreement?管理协议为期多久？

The agreement length and renewal terms are explained during your property assessment and set out in the signed management agreement.协议期限及续约条款会在物业评估时说明，并列明在签署的管理协议中。

When and how are owners paid?业主如何及何时收到款项？

WefieHome collects the platform payout on the owner's behalf and pays the owner monthly, together with the monthly owner report.WefieHome 代业主收取平台款项，每月向业主分账，并附上每月业主报告。

Where does WefieHome operate?WefieHome 在哪些地区运营？

WefieHome is based in Kuala Lumpur and serves property owners across Kuala Lumpur and the wider Klang Valley. Our current portfolio includes condominiums in Kuala Lumpur (including Cheras and Taman Pertama) and a landed house in Shah Alam.WefieHome 总部位于吉隆坡，服务吉隆坡及整个巴生谷的业主。目前的物业包括吉隆坡的公寓（包括蕉赖及 Taman Pertama）以及莎阿南的一间有地住宅。

What types of properties does WefieHome manage?WefieHome 管理哪些类型的物业？

We currently manage 18 active units across six properties: 2- and 3-bedroom condominium units in Kuala Lumpur and a 4-bedroom double-storey landed house in Shah Alam. Since 2018 we have managed more than 20 properties.我们目前管理六个物业中的 18 个活跃单位：吉隆坡的两房及三房公寓单位，以及莎阿南的一间四房双层有地住宅。自 2018 年以来，我们已管理超过 20 个物业。

How does the onboarding process work?合作流程是怎样的？

Enquiry and initial discussion, a site visit and property assessment (with a revenue projection, operating-cost assessment and setup budget), a proposal and management agreement, property preparation, listing launch, and then day-to-day operation with monthly reporting.咨询与初步沟通、实地查看与物业评估（包括收入预估、运营成本评估及布置预算）、方案与管理协议、物业准备、房源上线，之后进入日常运营并每月提供报告。
