Why it matters为什么重要

An empty home still needs someone to care.空置的房子，
也需要有人照顾。

  • Humidity & mould潮湿与发霉

    Closed-up rooms trap damp. Mould can spread quietly on walls, ceilings and furniture.门窗长期紧闭，湿气积聚，墙面、天花和家具可能悄悄发霉。

  • Leaks & hidden faults漏水与隐藏故障

    Plumbing, electrical and air-conditioning problems can start between visits.水管、电路和冷气的问题，往往在没人巡查时出现。

  • Pests & overgrown gardens虫害与杂草丛生

    Termites, pests and landscaping need attention before they become bigger problems.白蚁、虫害和庭院需要及早处理，以免小问题变大问题。

For owners who live elsewhere, travel often or own several properties, the real question is: who is making sure it’s properly looked after?对于住在别处、经常出差或拥有多处物业的业主，真正的问题是：谁在确保物业得到妥善照顾？

Is the rental income guaranteed?租金收入有保证吗？

No. Income depends on actual bookings and costs. The aim is an active, well-maintained and managed home.没有。收入取决于实际订单和开支。我们的目标是让物业保持活跃、得到维护和妥善管理。
