Essentials旅行须知

Everything you need from landing to getting around.从落地到市内出行，您需要知道的一切。

✈️

Arrival card (MDAC)入境卡（MDAC）

Most foreign visitors must submit the free Malaysia Digital Arrival Card online within 3 days before arrival (Singapore citizens are exempt). Use only the official Immigration website — avoid paid look-alike sites.大多数外国旅客须在抵达前 3 天内，免费在线填写马来西亚数字入境卡（新加坡公民免填）。请只使用移民局官方网站，避免付费的仿冒网站。

From KLIA / KLIA2 to the city从 KLIA / KLIA2 到市区

KLIA Ekspres: non-stop to KL Sentral in about 28 min (Terminal 1) or 33 min (Terminal 2), every 20 min, about 5am to 1am. RM55 one-way per adult (rising to RM65 from 1 Jan 2027); cheaper online and for groups/families. Skybus coach to KL Sentral is about RM13 and 75 min. Grab to the city is roughly RM80–130 and 45–75 min depending on traffic. With luggage, Grab door-to-door is usually easiest for our Cheras stays.KLIA Ekspres 机场快铁：直达 KL Sentral，一号航站楼约 28 分钟、二号航站楼约 33 分钟，每 20 分钟一班，约早上 5 点至凌晨 1 点。成人单程 RM55（2027 年 1 月 1 日起调至 RM65），网上购票及团体/家庭票更便宜。Skybus 巴士到 KL Sentral 约 RM13、约 75 分钟。Grab 到市区约 RM80–130，视交通约 45–75 分钟。带行李入住我们蕉赖一带的住宿，直接搭 Grab 通常最方便。

Getting around市内交通

MRT, LRT and Monorail run about 6am to midnight; fares are RM1.20–6.20. Bank cards can't be tapped at train gates yet, so buy a Touch 'n Go card (RM10 card, then top up) or a single-trip token from the station machine. Most of our Cheras stays are near the MRT Kajang Line, which goes straight to Bukit Bintang and Pasar Seni (Chinatown). Grab shows the fare before you book. The GO KL city bus costs RM1 per ride for visitors (children 12 and under free).MRT、LRT 和单轨火车约早上 6 点至午夜运行，车资 RM1.20–6.20。闸门目前还不能直接刷银行卡，请购买 Touch 'n Go 卡（卡费 RM10，再充值），或在车站售票机买单程代币。我们在蕉赖的大部分住宿靠近 MRT 加影线，可直达武吉免登和中央艺术坊（茨厂街）。Grab 下单前会显示车资。GO KL 市内巴士旅客每程 RM1（12 岁及以下儿童免费）。

📶

SIM card & eSIM电话卡与 eSIM

Tourist SIMs cost about RM18–35 for 7–15 days of data (CelcomDigi, Hotlink, U Mobile). Bring your passport to register. eSIMs are available. Airport counters are convenient but usually cost more than shops in the city.旅客电话卡 7–15 天流量约 RM18–35（CelcomDigi、Hotlink、U Mobile）。购买时须出示护照登记，也有 eSIM 可选。机场柜台方便，但通常比市区门店贵。

💳

Money & payments货币与付款

Currency is the ringgit (RM). Cards are widely accepted in malls and restaurants; keep some cash for hawker stalls and night markets. Many shops accept DuitNow QR, which also works with Alipay+ wallets and WeChat Pay. Restaurants often add a 10% service charge, so tipping isn't expected.货币为令吉（RM）。商场和餐厅普遍可刷卡；小贩摊和夜市请备些现金。许多商家接受 DuitNow QR，支付宝（Alipay+）和微信支付也可使用。餐厅通常已加收 10% 服务费，一般无须另给小费。

🌦️

Weather天气

Hot and humid all year, around 30°C. Short, heavy downpours are common in the late afternoon, so carry a small umbrella and plan indoor stops after 3pm. Malls and trains are strongly air-conditioned — a light layer helps.全年炎热潮湿，约 30°C。傍晚常有短暂的大雷雨，建议随身带折伞，下午 3 点后可安排室内行程。商场和地铁冷气很强，可带件薄外套。

🔌

Power & tap water电源与自来水

Plugs are type G (UK-style three-pin), 240V. Tap water is treated at the plant, but most locals boil or filter it before drinking — use the kettle or buy bottled water.插座为 G 型（英式三脚），240V。自来水在水厂经过处理，但本地人一般煮沸或过滤后才饮用——请用电热水壶烧开或购买瓶装水。

🆘

Emergency紧急电话

Police and ambulance: 999 (or 112 from a mobile). Fire: 994. KL Tourist Police: +603 2149 6590. For anything about your stay, WhatsApp WefieHome.警察及救护车：999（手机也可拨 112）。消防：994。吉隆坡旅游警察：+603 2149 6590。住宿相关问题请 WhatsApp WefieHome。

🙏

Local customs当地礼仪

At temples and mosques, cover shoulders and knees and take off your shoes; mosques lend robes and headscarves. Smoking and vaping are banned at all eateries, including open-air ones (fines up to RM5,000). Durian isn't allowed on trains and buses, and many condos and hotels don't allow it indoors.参观寺庙和清真寺请遮盖肩膀和膝盖，并脱鞋；清真寺会提供长袍和头巾。所有餐饮场所（包括露天）禁止吸烟和电子烟，最高罚款 RM5,000。榴莲不可带上火车和巴士，许多公寓和酒店也不允许带入室内。

Food美食

What to try必吃美食

  • Nasi lemak 椰浆饭Coconut rice with sambal, anchovies, peanuts and egg — the national dish.椰浆煮的饭配参巴辣酱、江鱼仔、花生和鸡蛋——国民美食。
  • Roti canai 印度煎饼Flaky griddled flatbread with dhal or curry, a mamak breakfast favourite.香脆的煎饼蘸豆咖喱或咖喱，嘛嘛档早餐首选。
  • Satay 沙爹Chargrilled chicken or beef skewers with peanut sauce.炭烤鸡肉或牛肉串，配花生酱。
  • Char kway teow 炒粿条Wok-fried flat rice noodles with prawns, cockles and egg.大火快炒的扁粿条，配虾、血蚶和鸡蛋。
  • KL Hokkien mee 福建面Thick noodles braised in dark soy — KL's own style, usually with pork and lard.黑酱油焖煮的粗面——吉隆坡特色，通常含猪肉和猪油渣。
  • Bak kut teh 肉骨茶Pork ribs simmered in a herbal broth (contains pork).猪排骨以药材汤熬煮（含猪肉）。
  • Banana leaf rice 香蕉叶饭Rice with vegetables, curries and papadum served on a banana leaf.米饭配蔬菜、咖喱和炸薄饼，盛在香蕉叶上。
  • Curry laksa 咖喱叻沙Spicy coconut-curry noodle soup with tofu puffs.香辣椰浆咖喱汤面，配豆卜。
  • Nasi kandar 扁担饭Rice with a mix of mamak curries poured over.米饭淋上多种嘛嘛档咖喱。

Can I pay for the MRT and LRT with my bank card?MRT 和 LRT 可以刷银行卡吗？

Is tap water safe to drink in Kuala Lumpur?吉隆坡的自来水可以直接喝吗？

Tap water is treated at the plant, but most locals boil or filter it before drinking. Use the kettle in your unit or buy bottled water.自来水在水厂经过处理，但本地人一般煮沸或过滤后才饮用。请使用单位里的电热水壶烧开，或购买瓶装水。
